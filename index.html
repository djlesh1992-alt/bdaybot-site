Three steps.

That's genuinely it.

1

Start a chat with Remembrly

Tap the button above, say hi, and save Remembrly as a contact. Everything happens in WhatsApp, no app, no download, no passwords.

2

Forward a contact card

Tap the + in WhatsApp, pick as many contacts as you like, and send. Remembrly asks each of them for their birthday or anniversary, so you don't have to.

3

Wake up to a reminder

Every morning a date arrives, you get a WhatsApp message at 7am local time with a one-tap link to message them directly. Job done.
